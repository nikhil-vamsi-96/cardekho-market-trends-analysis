# Analysis Results

## Dataset
- Records: 301
- Attributes: 9
- Columns: Car_Name, Year, Selling_Price, Present_Price, Kms_Driven, Fuel_Type, Seller_Type, Transmission, Owner

## Project Focus
- Price trends by manufacturing year
- Vehicle depreciation: Present_Price - Selling_Price
- Kilometres driven versus selling price
- Fuel-type distribution
- Transmission and seller-type distribution
- Ownership patterns
- Popular vehicle listings
- Car and two-wheeler comparison

## PPT Content
### Problem Statement
Analyze used-vehicle market data to identify pricing patterns, depreciation, and market trends.

### Project Description
This project analyzes CarDekho used-vehicle data to identify price trends, vehicle depreciation, and market patterns. It examines manufacturing year, selling price, present price, kilometres driven, fuel type, transmission, seller type, and ownership.

### End Users
- Used-vehicle buyers
- Individual sellers
- Dealers
- Automotive businesses
- Data analysts and students

### Technology Used
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook and CSV.
